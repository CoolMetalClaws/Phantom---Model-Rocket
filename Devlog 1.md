# New Project!

Phantom is my new project, a high soaring model rocket that stretches the upper bounds of a C-size motor at Mach 0.5! I made this rocket to improve upon my previous design and see how high a rocket can fly on a C6-5 motor.

## Sketch

As with all my other projects, i began this project by sketching out the rocket and how i wanted it to look, as well as deciding a name for it! The sketch also helped me decide what materials each component will be made of, where each part will be positioned, and how each component will be maufactured.

## CAD 

Based upon my simulated design and concept sketch, i began CADing the rocket on Onshape, as the nosecone will be 3D printed, and plan to continue adding an aerofoil shape to the fins.
